## Rules for boards

- **A pin belongs to exactly one board.** Re-pinning your own image to a second board is the
  duplicate-image spam signal; the engine gives every pin its own file instead.
- **Board order matters** on the profile — keep Senior Night first while it is in season
  (September–November, then January–February), Christmas ideas first in November.
- **Section a board only when it has 40+ pins.** Sections split the topic signal before that.
