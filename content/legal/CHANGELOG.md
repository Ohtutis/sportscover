# Legal document changelog

Every version of `/privacy`, `/privacy/biometric`, `/terms` and `/accessibility` is dated and kept.
The four pages carry the same version string in their record line, and a consent receipt names the
version that was in force when the order was placed. Previous versions are available on request at
hello@gamedayedition.com.

Version numbers are `YYYY-MM-<release>`; the effective date is the day the version went live.

| Version | Effective | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-F1 · effective September 7, 2026 | September 7, 2026 | First full drafts on the F1 site shell. Privacy: named subprocessor list (the direct-ordering providers marked as starting on the day direct ordering opens on this site), a retention table with real periods, GDPR legal bases, a COPPA position, the card page's unlisted default and the takedown route. Biometric: the likeness check written up for the Illinois, Texas and Washington biometric laws and GDPR Article 9, with consent, retention and destruction. Terms: licence and right of publicity, the two approvals, the refund ladder with the cancel-until-plate-approval right and the personalized-goods and digital-content withdrawal waivers, delivery dates counted from the order date, the five-year registry pledge and the wind-down promise, Lithuanian law. Accessibility: the WCAG 2.2 AA statement, known limits and the contact route. |

## House rules for the next version

1. Bump the version string in all four page files together — they must never disagree.
2. Add a row here before the pages ship; the summary says what changed for the reader, not which
   paragraph moved.
3. Keep the previous text. A consent receipt has to be able to point at what was accepted.
4. The imprint block renders only when the environment carries a complete imprint; until then the
   pages say the studio is operated from Lithuania and give the contact address. Never a placeholder
   label, never "details coming".
