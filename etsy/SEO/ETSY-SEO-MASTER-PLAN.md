# Etsy SEO master plan — every listing, every keyword, one map

**Written 2026-08-28, revised same day after the full ~55-term sweep** (see
`NICHE-REPORT-2026-08.md` for the complete atlas and 12-month curves). Raw earlier evidence:
`ETSY-SEO-FINDINGS.md`. Shop: **GameDayEdition**.

**The niche runs on three seasonal engines: senior night (Oct 6475 + Jan 3267), CHRISTMAS
(football gifts Nov 26.7k — 2.5x any other spike) and SPRING baseball (baseball mom Mar 27.8k;
custom baseball card 16.6k/yr High conv, peaks BOTH Dec and May). The waves below are aligned
to all three, not just senior night.**

**The four rules that govern everything below**

1. **One intent = one listing.** Every search phrase has exactly one owning listing (§6).
   Two of our listings competing for one phrase = both lose.
2. **The sport is the doorway, the pack is the price decision.** Sport-specific listings
   catch the search; the set/pack listings monetize the convinced.
3. **Tags are ≤20 characters.** Long phrases (`football senior night`, 21) live in TITLES;
   tags carry the ≤20 building blocks Etsy recombines.
4. **Once a listing has impressions — freeze title/tags 30 days.** Iterate on images/price,
   not on the index.

## 4. Description skeleton (all listings)

1. **Opener** (≤160 chars, exact head phrase + "not a template / designed for you") — §3.
2. **What you get** — the exact deliverables, counted (poster sizes, card front+back, 300 DPI).
3. **How it works** — send photos → we design → proof to approve → files. State the turnaround
   and, on SN listings: "ordering for a specific senior night date? Tell us the date."
4. **Why it's different** — 4 unique shots, numbered + registered edition (QR), any club colors.
5. **Print freedom** — home / print shop / online lab.
6. FAQ: likeness, revisions, deadlines, team orders (→ link the set/team listing).
