## Reading rules

1. Tier 1 phrases go in TITLES of their owner listing; ≤20-char forms go in tags.
2. Tier 3 volume terms are TAG fillers on the matching listing only — never the title lead.
3. A * next to conversion = the rating is distorted by season or by a different product
   owning the shelf (check the bestsellers before copying the phrase).
4. Word order matters: `senior night volleyball` (High) ≠ `volleyball senior night` (Low) —
   put both orders across title + tags on the volleyball SN listing.
