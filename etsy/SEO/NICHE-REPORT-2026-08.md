## 4. Competitive archetypes (who actually owns these shelves)

1. **Photo-collage / done-for-you** — the giant: LetsMakeMemoriesUS photo banner $24.19 /
   **32.5k reviews**; CarolinaCustPrinting SN poster board $33.88/1k; JCSDesigns $45.50/**27.6k**;
   CalitheMakerShop photo letter $27.82/4.7k (per-sport variants of ONE product).
2. **Canva templates** — SeasonSuiteSports SN poster bundles $18/1.1k per sport;
   collage templates $3–7 with 475–1.4k reviews; `sports card template` 93/High/+186%.
3. **Party consumables** — leis $27–52 (9.7k reviews), sashes $14.52/11.4k, buttons, fans,
   big-head cutouts $12/10.7k. Senior-night median purchase $8.10–9.90 = this stuff.
4. **Premium one-offs** — hand-painted display $145/523 reviews; acrylic collage $63.53.

**We are none of these — designed-for-you AI sports art is a distinct fourth archetype.**
Priced above templates and party stuff, at/above collage, below hand-painted. Every listing's
first line must draw the boundary: *"Not a template, not a collage — we design the artwork
for you from your photos."*

## 7. Method notes for the next sweep

- Search is URL-driven: `/your/shops/me/marketplace-insights/search?query=<term>` (no typing).
- 12-month view is a UI toggle only (no URL param found) — Date range → Last 12 months.
- Similar-terms tables are paginated (9–20 pages) — the free harvest is bigger than the query.
- Plus limits: none hit in ~55 queries.
- Always sanity-check WHAT the shelf sells (bestsellers block) before adopting a term —
  `senior banner`'s Very-high conv belongs to $6 bucket-list templates, not to us.

**Two peaks, twelve-month curves measured:**
- `fantasy football draft board` — Aug 2026 **4,831** vs 31–200 the rest of the year. A July–August
  product, nothing else. Sep already collapsed to 654.
- `fantasy football trophy` — **Dec 10,242 + Jan 5,377** (championship) AND Aug 8,885 (draft).
  ~36k/year on 7.8k listings.

**Price and competitors.** Draft kit median purchase **$15.30–18.70**; bestsellers are small shops
(DraftExpert $31.45 / 73 reviews; another at 38 reviews) — the category is NOT review-locked, which
is why a new shop can enter. Trophy median purchase **$33.30–40.70**, but every seller there ships a
**physical engraved trophy** ($30 SKTrophy → $179 TrophySmack). Selling paper into that term is the
risk.

**Fit with our production:** a draft board is a large printed sheet + hundreds of player labels —
our Printful poster sizes and die-cut sticker pipeline already do exactly this, and no AI athlete
art is needed. The championship award is a weaker fit (buyers want an object).
