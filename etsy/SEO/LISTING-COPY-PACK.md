House rules baked into all copy below:
- **Physical listings show a shipping-based delivery date even for the digital package** (processing + transit to the VIEWER's country — a Lithuanian browser sees ~10 days more than a US buyer). Every DIGITAL package line therefore ends with: `The delivery date Etsy shows applies to printed packages only — digital files arrive by Etsy message in 1–2 business days.` Applied to all 19 live listings 2026-09-04.
- **Every title word must be a MEASURED phrase or do a job.** Two words that failed this test
  and are banned from titles: `keepsake` (sports keepsake = **4 searches**, baseball keepsake
  260/Very low — it only earns its place in a SENIOR context) and `from your photos`
  (not a search term at all — it is the hero's job and the description's first line).
  Words that did pass and are worth spending: `teen boy gifts` **1.5k**, `athlete gift` 265,
  plus the sport's own exact head phrase.
- **Title ≤14 WORDS and NO WORD USED TWICE** — Etsy's form warns on both ("Consider using 14
  words or less", "Try to avoid repetition"). Repetition buys no ranking: Etsy dedupes, so a
  second `football` or `card` only costs a slot. Put the exact head phrase first, then spend
  every remaining word on a DIFFERENT concept.
  Word-savers that cost no ranking: drop `From Your Photos` (nobody searches it — it lives in
  the hero and the first description line), use `and` not `&`, one `Custom` per title.
- Tags all ≤20 chars, delivered as ONE comma-separated line; NO `personalized card`
  (greeting-card shelf), NO bare `football cards` (Panini shelf).
- Senior/graduation tags live ONLY on senior-night listings; evergreens stay evergreen.
- Every description opens ≤160 chars with the head phrase + "not a template" boundary.
- Prices per the decided ladder: card $49 · poster $49 · sets $69.99–89 (LAUNCH sale on top).
  Banner price proposal: $34.99 (between template $18 and done-for-you board $34–50). ⚠️ confirm.
