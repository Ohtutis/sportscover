## 2 · Two title shapes, and why they differ

- **Where an exact head phrase is measured** — baseball, basketball, football — keep
  `Custom <Sport> Card` **contiguous**. Inserting `Trading` between them breaks the exact
  phrase Etsy is matching.
- **Where there is no head to protect** — cheerleading, volleyball — write
  `Custom <Sport> Trading Card` and borrow `custom trading card` (2.1k) instead.

Soccer keeps `Teen Athlete`, not `Teen Boy Athlete`: its buyers are split boy/girl and the
narrower phrase would cost half the shelf. Volleyball and cheer use `Teen Girl`.

## 3 · Two corrections to what is written elsewhere

**`Digital Download` → `Printed or Digital`.** `LISTING-COPY-PACK.md` was written 2026-08-28,
before the V3 rollout turned every listing Physical with three printed tiers. A title promising
a digital download on a listing whose top tier ships cards is wrong on the shelf and wrong in
search. The live listings already carry the new tail; the three new ones adopt it from the start.

**All three live titles drop `From Your Photos`.** Checked in the editor on 2026-09-02 — every
one of them spends three words on it. The README's own house rule bans the phrase: it is not a
search term, it is the hero's job and the description's first line. Those three words now carry
`For Teen Boy Athlete` / `For Teen Girl` — `teen boy gifts` is **1.5k/mo measured**, and the
phrase is currently in only one of the three live card listings.

## 4 · The `ready/` files were stale — all six rewritten

`ready/football-card.md` and `ready/cheerleading-card.md` dated 2026-08-28 and still described
the **pre-V3 world**: `Type: Digital`, a single price of 50.81, and a `DIGITAL PRODUCT — no
physical item is shipped with this listing` block. Publishing from them would have created a
listing that contradicts its own variants.

All six are now written against the live V3 structure: Physical type, four `Package` variants
with per-variant processing, US-only free shipping, Simple 30-day returns, Bay + QPMN partners,
and a `SHIPPING` block in place of `DIGITAL PRODUCT`. The `OUR PROMISE` block added on
2026-09-02 is in all of them — the refund promise is made on listing image 17, and a promise in
an image that is missing from the description is an uncovered claim.

## 5 · What is NOT in these listings, deliberately

- **No senior or graduation tags.** One intent, one owner (README §2) — those belong to the
  senior-night listings.
- **No `personalized card`** (greeting-card shelf) and **no bare `<sport> cards`** (Panini
  collector shelf).
- **No new video.** The three live listings already have one and keep it; the three new ones
  publish without, rather than reusing another sport's.
- **No `keepsake`** outside a senior context — `sports keepsake` measures 4 searches.
