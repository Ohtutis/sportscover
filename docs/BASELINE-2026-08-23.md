## How to read the node lines

```
name | type | x | y | w | h [| fontSize | align | autoResize | boundVariable] [| imageHash | fillOpacity | fillVariable]
```
`type` is the first letter (R=rectangle, T=text, F=frame). `align`/`autoResize` are first letters
(L/C/R, W=WIDTH_AND_HEIGHT, H=HEIGHT, N=NONE). Blank fields mean "not set / default".

## When the customer has edited: how to read the diff

Re-capture the same nodes with the snapshot script (`art-pipeline/figma/validate-layout.js`
sits next to it; the capture is the read-only block in this session's history) and compare
line by line. What a change means:

| What moved | Read it as |
|---|---|
| a `#photo_*` slot's x/y/w/h | a composition decision **for this athlete** — do not copy it to a sport whose poses have different aspects; copy the PRINCIPLE (narrow pose to the tall side) |
| a nameplate's y or font size | a typographic decision for that FINISH — the finishes have different fonts, so the point size never transfers literally, only the proportion (surname fills the plate) |
| a fill opacity on a ghost | a material decision for that finish; ghosts run 0.04–0.6 and each finish has its own level |
| a `team/primary-ink` binding switched back to `team/primary` | the customer wants that element dark — the contrast rule was not meant to apply there |
| a text's x with the alignment unchanged | the anchor moved deliberately; do not "correct" it back to the master |

**Before assuming a change is a mistake, look at the artboard.** Three times in this session a
numeric rule said something was wrong when the picture said it was right, and each of those
"fixes" had to be reverted.

**What it means.** The lockup gets more air, the supporting figures lift clear of the type and
gain a little weight, and the hero's face sits higher and slightly larger on the card — the same
instinct that drove the avatar reframe. The hero on the POSTER was deliberately left alone: it
was already right there.

**What transferred and what did not.** The slot fractions and the spacing transferred to all six
finishes, because those are one layout holding the same three images. Nothing about type size
transferred — the finishes have different fonts and each keeps its own scale.

**One correction made while propagating:** nameplate spacing is not a constant. Writing a flat 9
into the print export (which is 4.167× scale) collapsed a 25 px gap; it has to be
`9 × replicaWidth / 760`.


## Revision 2026-08-24 (b) — the ghost numeral, everywhere

Customer: *"the number goes centred behind the main hero photo and is like the hero poster."*

Measured on the reference poster, the numeral's relationship to the hero is:

| | value |
|---|---|
| ink centre, horizontally | **the hero subject's own centre** |
| ink centre, vertically | **62 % down the hero subject** |
| ink height | **0.72 × the hero subject height** on posters, socials and wallpapers |
| ink height | **0.51 ×** on card fronts — the card keeps its quieter ghost |

Applied to every board on all six finishes (78 in total). Before the pass the numeral sat
anywhere from 100 px left to 99 px right of the hero and its height ran 0.51–0.77 with no rule.

**The family is chosen by BOARD TYPE, never by whatever the ratio happened to be.** The first
attempt classified by the current ratio, which split the four phone wallpapers between the two
families for no reason other than where they had drifted to.

Heritage's `number_block_shadow` and Prism Rush's `number_echo_*` move and resize with their
numeral — the shadow at 10/9, the echoes at 1:1. A numeral that moves without them breaks the
effect.

Motion-spec sheets are excluded: they are annotation surfaces, not designs.
