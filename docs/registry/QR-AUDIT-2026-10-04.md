# Registry QR audit — listing images, 2026-10-04 (ticket F1-ART-09)

Started 2026-10-04, completed 2026-10-06 (a machine restart in between cost one run; the evidence below
is the complete second run). Script: `scripts/registry-qr-audit.ts` (new). Records: `lib/registry/cards.ts`
(72 added), `lib/registry/art.ts` (`ART_PENDING`, ticket F1-ART-09), `lib/registry/art-sources.ts`
(pending targets), `public/cards/qr/` (72 new PNGs).

## The finding this closes

`etsy/LISTING-STATE.md`, 2026-09-14 (tęsinys 7): every live card listing shows the BACK of each finish at a
readable size — each back printing its own card id, e.g. `GDE-FS-ICH-2026-17` — while the registry held only
the one or two lead ids per sport. A buyer who reads or scans one of the other backs gets
"NO CARD REGISTERED UNDER THIS ID". `tests/registry.test.ts` already failed on `GDE-FS-ICH-2026-17`.

## How the evidence was collected

```bash
npx tsx scripts/registry-qr-audit.ts etsy/listing-images "Exportai Etsy" --ocr --jobs 6 \
  --cache work/registry-qr-audit/cache.json --json … --md …        # decode every image (+ OCR of QR images)
npx tsx scripts/registry-qr-audit.ts etsy/listing-images "Exportai Etsy" --ocr-all \
  --cache work/registry-qr-audit/cache.json --md …                 # OCR every image, from the cache
```

- **Scope — 4,153 images, every PNG / JPG / WebP in both trees** (both are gitignored local working copies):
  `etsy/listing-images/**` (2,607: the Figma sources under `<section>/src/`, plus the older slide exports at
  section top level) and `Exportai Etsy/**` (1,546: the slides as uploaded to Etsy, one folder per listing —
  17 card, 17 poster, 9 Senior Night set, 11 + 8 banner, blankets, complete set, digital). Nothing outside
  the repo was read.
- **iCloud:** 1,021 images were evicted; each was hydrated right before it was read (`cat <file> >/dev/null`,
  then `find <file> -flags +dataless` must print nothing), strictly one at a time — mean 1.2 s, max 6 s, no
  failure. All 4,153 images decoded; none unreadable.
- **QR decode (jsqr + sharp):** whole image (≤ 2000 px), four overlapping quadrants, then 900 px and 450 px
  windows at half-window stride (two backs side by side defeat jsqr's finder search on a whole slide); each
  symbol is painted out and the view decoded again. 334 zero-byte "symbols" jsqr finds in textured art were
  dropped (nothing GDE prints is empty).
- **Printed ids (macOS Vision OCR through `osascript`):** every image, plus an up-to-4× crop beside each of
  the 535 symbols. The QR only proves where a phone goes; the printed id is what a buyer types into
  `/registry/lookup` — and on these listings the two disagree (below).

1. **Every QR decodes to a registered card — but mostly the wrong one.** 535 symbols in 438 images, 24
   distinct card ids, 0 unregistered. 431 of the 535 symbols (357 images: 165 Etsy exports, 192 repo images)
   encode `GDE-SN-BKB-2026-23` — Nia Brooks, basketball: the QR baked into the card art of every finish of
   every sport file, and into the certificates and proofs. Of the 400 symbols whose printed id could be read,
   **330 (in 264 images) encode a different card than the id printed beside them** — 329 of them Nia's card.
   This is F1-QR-01 (`docs/SITE-F1-HANDOFF.md` item 1), still open: until the sport files' `#qr` layer is
   rebound to each card's own QR and the listings are re-exported, a buyer who scans a non-lead back lands on
   Nia's page, not on the card in the photo. The full list is the section "QR encodes a different card than
   the id printed beside it" below — it is the re-export list.
2. **The printed ids are where the registry was short.** 129 distinct ids are printed across the images; 90
   did not resolve before this change:

## Closing F1-ART-09, and keeping this true

- Per card: rebind the back's `#qr` layer to the card's own QR (`public/cards/qr/<id>.png`; today every back
  on disk encodes `GDE-SN-BKB-2026-23`), export front + back square-cut from the sport file, drop them on the
  `lib/registry/art-sources.ts` path, delete the entry from `ART_PENDING`, run `npm run cards:assets`.
- Before publishing any listing: `npx tsx scripts/registry-qr-audit.ts etsy/listing-images` — exit 1 names an
  unregistered QR id and the image (add `"Exportai Etsy"` to check the uploaded exports too). On a Mac,
  `--ocr` also lists the printed ids; read that list by eye — OCR misreads small type, so it never fails the
  run. `--cache <file>` makes a re-run instant; this audit's cache, JSON and logs are in
  `work/registry-qr-audit/` (gitignored, safe to delete).
